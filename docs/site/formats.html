All artifacts use the group ID io.github.nalukit. The optional PDF modules are found at runtime through ServiceLoader; there is nothing to configure. A PDF method called without the module raises an IllegalStateException that names the missing dependency.

Invoices & credit notes

Read and write UBL 2.1 and CII D16B at document, party, and line level. Validate EN 16931 and XRechnung 3.0.2 with the packaged KoSIT rules; documents stating XRechnung 1.2 to 2.3 are checked against the last release of their version. The XRechnung extension adds sub invoice lines and third party payments.

Hybrid invoices

Write, read, and validate Factur-X / ZUGFeRD PDF/A-3 in all six profiles with the packaged FeRD rules (ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 / Factur-X 1.09.2), with attachments and producer detection. embedXml embeds existing CII byte for byte. ZUGFeRD 1.0 is read, validated, and migrated to ZUGFeRD 2.

Profile EXTENDED & France

EXTENDED adds further parties, structured payment terms, logistics service charges, advance payments, sub-lines, and product details. The French profile EXTENDED-CTC-FR is written, read, and validated with the EXTENDED-CTC-FR Schematron and the BR-FR rules (France_RFE 1.4.0.04).

Orders & delivery notes

Order-X 1.0 orders, order changes, and responses (BASIC, COMFORT, EXTENDED with the COMFORT content) with the packaged official rules. Despatch advices after Peppol BIS 3, as 1Lieferschein, and as CIDA delivery notes in XML and PDF/A-3.

Reading received documents

Strict by default; ReadMode.LENIENT reads documents whose amounts the model does not reproduce and reports each deviation. importInvoice keeps the original XML and stated amounts, and export returns the original or the normalized document.

Validation findings

Findings carry rule ID, severity, XPath, and stage, plus an archivable XML report. Optional: calculation check (PANIOLO-CALC-*), rule statistics for audits, an acceptance policy for blocking warnings, and Mustang-compatible reporting of unknown documents.

Rendering & viewing

paniolo-pdf-layout renders DIN 5008 invoices, orders, delivery notes, and validation reports as PDF/A-3b in German, English, and French, with logo, own fonts, accent color, and footer. paniolo-visualizer shows documents in the browser with <paniolo-viewer>.

Command line & migration

paniolo-cli validates, inventories, reads, converts, renders, and visualizes documents in scripts and CI, with exit codes and JSON output. paniolo-mustang maps Mustang invoices to the model and names every value it cannot carry over.

Know the boundaries

  • Rendered documents can be customized with logo, fonts, colors, and footer; for your own designs you provide the visual PDF/A document and Paniolo embeds the XML. Arbitrary PDFs are not converted to PDF/A.
  • Transmission through Peppol access points, email, or portals such as ZRE or OZG-RE is outside the library, as are recipient-specific requirements beyond the official rules.
  • Official Peppol Despatch Advice rules must be supplied separately.
  • New ZUGFeRD 1.0 documents are not written; XRechnung 1.0 and 1.1 are validated against the current rules.
  • CIDA validation is unavailable because no schema is published. Some Order-X EXTENDED and despatch advice details are outside the model.
  • Java versions below 21 are not supported.
